# COO / human handoff checklist

Producer provides unchecked items and evidence paths. COO/human gate writes the verdict.

## Per ~10 s segment

- [ ] Real preview and ≥2 relevant current-version stills viewed at 1920×1080; critical action strips supplied.
- [ ] POV/style grammar followed; one small learning action; no next-segment spoiler.
- [ ] Main subject large/readable; zero overlap among text/subjects/arrows/brackets/subtitles, including camera endpoints.
- [ ] Visual claims match the verified KLP; all promised variables/rules explained, no invented IDs or borrowed demo story.
- [ ] VO listened to at normal speed; text/voice/subtitle sync and pronunciation known; no duplicated voice.
- [ ] `TREATMENT`, current `timeline.json`, editable `src`, checks and `RUN` are present; failures disclosed.
- [ ] Trial/reviewer disclaimer if brief requires it; real director/backend/session identified.
- [ ] `COO-GATE.md` remains empty; next segment not started without explicit external segment approval.

## Full film

- [ ] Actual `film.mp4` full-speed audiovisual viewing; at least three current output key PNGs and a contact sheet.
- [ ] Continuous camera/scene, timing, sure ending frame, required read-holds, no empty/NaN/residual/overlapped frames.
- [ ] Duration 30–45 s, 1920×1080; fps/codecs/audio channels, measured loudness/hash, full decode recorded.
- [ ] `film.srt` (if specified), `poster`, source, `build.sh`/documented steps, `CREDITS` and asset licences included.
- [ ] `evidence/RUN.md`: actual model ID, session, runtime/environment, dependencies, commands, verified checks and remaining limitations.
- [ ] Any media/talk tracks from sample/reference are excluded unless rights were explicit; no proprietary README/demo-only labels leaked.
- [ ] `FULL-GATE.md` stays empty until reviewer fills it. No implied teacher/student/handwriting/DeepSeek-film PASS.
