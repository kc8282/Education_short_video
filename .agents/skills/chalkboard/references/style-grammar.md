# Chalkboard grammar

Based on the upstream chalkboard style grammar, distilled for new educational topics. Preserve full upstream attribution when copying/adapting source text or code.

## Core semantics

| Device | Teaching use | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| Centre-line stroke growth | how a symbol/diagram/argument forms | typeset text fading/wiping in as a mask |
| White structural chalk | question, invariant, relationships | decoration |
| One accent chalk | the same conceptual quantity/part through transformations | random highlights, all arrows |
| Pale second voice | pupil claim or predictable wrong path | warning from an arbitrary authority |
| One wipe + same-position correction | a specific misconception understood by replacement | cosmetic eraser, data vanishing |
| Rule/parameter traced across equation steps | covariance, conservation, root, constant, cause | drawing conspicuous coloured boxes everywhere |
| Slow pan on a series | sequence in time or derivation | speed-watching unreadable tiny terms |
| Same frame later | judgement before/after | unrelated new camera for the answer |
| Sliding board (optional) | hidden derivation or next problem | slide/page-flip graphic |
| Room reveal | classroom setting, whole problem context | cinematic establishing shot wasting teaching time |

## Composition and colour

One dark, almost evenly ink-green slate, slight grain, warm wall/wood frame and tray. Only faint physical light and no dirty old chalk swirls, dust bokeh, glow, floating HUD or gratuitous icons. White ink for structure; a single restrained warmer colour for tracked subject; at most a pale wrong/other voice. General ink grain must not make any stroke <3 px on 1080p.

The camera is front-on and flat; no fake perspective blackboard. A key equation/diagram occupies at least one third of the height, generally much more. Its safe box contains its strokes, underline and follow-on labels, not just a text-font metric. One sentence can need two panels if it exceeds safe readable width. Optional 6–8% pushes and slow pans only while preserving text/subject bounds.

## Writing and time

Use hand-authored stroke centre lines (position, type, pressure and width profile) for native handwriting needs. Compare standard stroke order and character shapes with a knowledgeable reader; checking over an outline is only a test aid. Missing Chinese glyphs must not silently skip while building; either explicitly supply path data or disclose a simpler labelled diagram route.

A writing stroke reveals by arc length, with a lightly grainy deposit and sparse lift-off grains—not a glowing leading point. Big characters are written at a natural rhythm; if 40 s can't fit reading and movement, reduce board wording and move explanation to voice.

One right-to-left (or geometrically appropriate) wipe is a progressive soft-edge destination-out mask clipped **only to the wrong layer**. Record start/middle/end as real frames; residual wrong pixels are a defect. The correction is an own `post` layer in the same coordinate slot. Structural content outside must be unchanged.

## Sound and reading

Sparse chalk sounds matched to actual movement, restrained room tone, short optional plucked notes, quiet voice. Voice names each part in plain language when first written. Any licensed recordings are sourced and credited; no inherited demo soundtrack. Subtitle is a separate lower safe-area plate, not more on-board clutter. Board reading budget: ≥CJK/4.5 + other/15 + 1.5 s after full visibility. Actual reading and listening can still reject a technically valid check.
